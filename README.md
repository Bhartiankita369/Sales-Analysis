# Retail Store Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail store sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand overall sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, payment preferences, and channel (in-store vs. online) trends. An interactive dashboard was built to help visualize key metrics and support data-driven business decisions.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall retail sales performance
- Understand sales distribution across product categories
- Compare in-store vs. online sales performance
- Study payment method preferences among customers
- Track sales trends over time
- Build an interactive dashboard for easy data exploration

## Technologies Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Dashboard Design

## Key Metrics (KPIs)
- **Total Sales:** 1,552,071
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 66,276
- **Total Transactions:** 12,576
- **Average Order Value:** 123.32
- **Discount Applied:** 24.45%

## Analysis Performed
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Sales by category analysis
- Sales by payment method analysis
- Sales by location (in-store vs. online) analysis
- Sales trend analysis over time (year-wise)
- Category-wise comparison across sales channels

## Dashboard Features
- **Sales by Category:** Bar chart comparing total sales across categories such as Beverages, Butchers, Computers and Electric Accessories, Electric Household Essentials, Food, Furniture, Milk Products, and Patisserie
- **Sales by Payment Method:** Pie chart showing the split between Cash and Credit Card transactions
- **Sales by Location:** Pie chart comparing In-store vs. Online sales
- **Sales Trend Over Time:** Line chart tracking sales across years (2022–2025)
- **Sales by Category and Location:** Grouped bar chart comparing in-store and online sales for each category
- **Interactive Slicers:** Filter data dynamically by Year, Payment Method, Category, and Location

## Insights
- Sales are fairly evenly distributed across most product categories, with Electric Household Essentials and Butchers among the top performers
- Payment method usage is nearly balanced across Cash, Credit Card, and other methods (~33–34% each)
- Online sales (51%) slightly edge out in-store sales (49%), indicating a strong digital sales channel
- Sales showed a declining trend in the most recent period (2025), which may warrant further investigation into demand or seasonality factors
- Category-wise channel comparison helps identify which product categories perform better online vs. in-store, supporting inventory and marketing decisions
